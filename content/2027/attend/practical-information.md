## Travel information

### How to get to Vienna

[Vienna International Airport (VIE)](https://www.viennaairport.com/en/) is about 20 km from the city centre and connected to it by train (S-Bahn, Railjet and the City Airport Train). Vienna is also well connected by rail to the rest of Europe; see [ÖBB](https://www.oebb.at/en/).
